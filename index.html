BIO BLAZERS DEVELOPMENT NOTES

SAPLING DROPPERS · UPDATE LOG

CURRENT BUILD · 1.6.9
RESULT SCREEN TRANSLATIONS

Added translations for score summaries, game-over details, victory banners, timer and wind readouts, and field-report pages.

MULTILINGUAL SUPPORT

Added English, Spanish, French and German options. The selected language is saved on this device and updates the game controls, HUD, mission messages and field report.

UPDATE LOG ORDER

Reorganized the development history so the newest changes appear first and the original game appears last.

SKILL SCORING & PERSONAL BEST

Added different rewards for soil preparation, precise sapling planting, and seedbomb hits. Challenge gives 1.5× points and Gale gives 2×. Accurate landings, time remaining, and clean stages earn bonuses; misses subtract points. A personal high score is saved in this browser.

UPDATE LOG

Added a scrollable development history to the cover, with its own button, close control, and Escape-key support.

CHATGPT CREDIT

Added a tiny bottom-right cover credit: the game was made by ChatGPT with prompts from a human, alongside a deliberately clunky little logo.

TITLE COVER

Added the Bio Blazers cover screen, pixel-art drone and forest scene, and a large Play button before the campaign begins.

LEVEL 2 DRONE

Designed a separate olive-and-gold seed-spreader drone for the seedbomb stage, while keeping the original planting drone for Level 1.

CHUNKIER SEEDBOMB AIR PUFF

Reduced the puff to fewer, larger square particles for a bolder pixel-cloud effect.

ROCK HAZARDS

Added dangerous rocks to each stage. Landing on one counts as a miss. Reduced the rock count from eight to four and increased spacing so the rocks feel less crowded.

SEEDBOMB AIR PUFF

Added an animated pale air puff whenever the drone releases a seedbomb.

BIO BLAZERS BRANDING

Changed the creator credit from By the Bark Knights to By the Bio Blazers.

BIOME EXPERIMENT · ROLLED BACK

Tested tropical, desert and snowy environments with different trees. The alternate tree styles did not match the original simple look, so the experiment was reverted and the classic forest was restored.

GLOBAL PLAY COUNTER

Connected the game to a shared counter using a Vercel API route and Upstash storage, so visitors can see the global number of campaign starts.

MOBILE & DOWNLOAD SUPPORT

Added touch controls for phones and tablets, responsive layouts, keyboard controls, and a button to download a standalone copy of the game.

TWO-LEVEL CAMPAIGN

Added the Level 1 forest-growth celebration and a Level 2 seedbomb trial. The campaign ends with a one-tree seedbomb cutscene and a field report explaining the project's illustrative survival-rate comparison.

WIND, DIFFICULTY & TERRAIN

Added Normal, Challenge and Gale wind profiles, changing gusts for individual falling objects, a mission timer, three-miss limit, and randomized hills and planting-marker positions.

THE ORIGINAL GAME

Started as a retro 2D drone reforestation game. Fly across the field, prepare eight ground sites with soil pods, then plant saplings and grow a new forest.
